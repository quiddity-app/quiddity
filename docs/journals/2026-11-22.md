- Keep the main thread clear while typing, searching and completing ([#517](https://github.com/quiddity-app/quiddity/pull/517))
	- Kept the main thread clear of work a keystroke needn't wait for. [[Performance]] [[The UI]]
		- Saving can't be starved: the push to the shell and the notification of typing both wait for quiet but never more than a second, where the push used to wait for typing to stop. [[Publish gate]]
		- A read made again shares what didn't change, so a block that hasn't changed isn't drawn again; leaving an unchanged block is no longer an edit.
		- The sidebar and references are read in a deferred render; search, [[All entities]] and link completion follow what is typed a moment behind it. [[Search]] [[Editing]]
		- Measured, and the gain is small. The lag in adding, indenting and outdenting wasn't reproduced; the index rebuilt after a structural edit (about 220 ms native at 3,150 pages) is the likelier cause and is Rust. Measure in the app before changing more.
- Let a property follow another, so a class holds its subclasses' instances ([#523](https://github.com/quiddity-app/quiddity/pull/523))
	- Added `##through(property)` to [[Built-in properties]]: effective values follow another property, and [[Queries]] see them.
		- The property it goes through must be a `ref`. A `parent` with no meta-properties is text and has nothing to follow, which is easy to forget; it isn't flagged yet.
		- Subclass lists come from `Index::through_children`, built beside the inverse map in `compute_effective`.
	- An inverse tree nests indirect instances under their subclass, and `InverseNode::direct` says which nodes are written.
