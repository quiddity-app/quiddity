- Plan history kept apart from Loro's op log ([#476](https://github.com/quiddity-app/quiddity/pull/476))
	- Planned keeping a coarse history indefinitely, apart from [[Loro]]'s op log, which is kept only as long as [[Sync]] needs it. [[History]]
		- Commits are one per block edit, folded into runs on one entity after an hour and into sessions after a day. They are kept in lanes per device, so that two devices editing at once don't interleave. Tidying is by rules that look only at a commit and its neighbours, so devices that tidy concurrently agree.
		- No wall clocks decide anything. A device's horizon, the heads another device knows it has seen, replaces its last-seen time, and eviction is by a budget of changes beyond it. [[Compaction]]
		- A device that comes back after eviction rebases its edits and has its late commits rewritten, which answers the open question in [[Design]].
		- Existing graphs will be migrated offline and the other devices synced again from blank. The page lists the pull requests in order.
