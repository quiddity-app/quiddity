- What Quiddity is meant to feel familiar beside, or borrowed from: the entities with `type:: prior art`, listed in the references below. Someone who has used an outliner like Logseq, Obsidian or Roam should find outliner editing, pages, journals, `[[links]]`, `#tags` and backlinks where they expect them. [[Design]]
- What Quiddity does differently: every device holds the whole graph and syncs peer to peer, with no service in the middle, and properties are typed by data in the graph rather than by code.
