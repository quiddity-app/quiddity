- How Quiddity is built, and how to work on it. Using it is in the [[User guide]]. The repository's `AGENTS.md` files have the working notes; these pages have the reasoning.
- Start here
	- [[Design]]: the goals, the decisions and the open questions.
	- [[Architecture]]: the app, the headless node and the crates.
	- [[Running the app]], [[Testing]], [[Linting and formatting]], [[Releases]] and [[Updates]].
	- [[Performance]]: what costs time in a large graph, and what is still to do.
	- [[Stats and debug]]: numbers about a graph, and a check that what is derived from it agrees with it.
	- [[Network log]]: what the network has lately done, for working out why devices don't sync.
	- [[First run]]: how a device with no graph begins, and joins one.
	- [[Background]]: what happens when the app is put away and comes back, and what is not built.
- The parts
	- Crates: what each does is in [[Architecture]], and they are listed under [[Crate]].
	- Apps: [[The UI]] and [[The shell]]. How they look: the [[Design language]].
	- What they are built on is listed under [[Library]], and what they are built with under [[Tool]]. [[Licences]] says how the licences of all of it are collected and shipped.
- Between devices
	- [[Sync]]: the protocol, over [[iroh]].
	- [[Device list]]: who may connect.
	- [[Identity]]: device keys, graph IDs and node IDs, built in part. [[Copied graphs]]: noticing that a graph was copied.
	- [[App sync]]: how the desktop app joins in.
	- [[Presence]]: who is here, and which blocks they have open.
	- [[Sync status]]: how a device stands with the others, as the app's cloud shows it.
	- [[Background sync]]: syncing while the app isn't open, planned.
	- [[Publish gate]]: holding back typing nobody can see.
	- [[Blob transfer]]: fetching the bytes of binaries from another device.
	- [[Compaction]]: what history each device keeps.
	- [[History]]: planned, keeping a coarse history indefinitely and dropping Loro's op log, so that a device away too long can still come back.
	- [[Duplicates]]: merging what two devices made at once.
- On disk
	- [[Storage]]: the graph's file, and what is derived.
	- [[Versioning]]: changing the format without breaking stored graphs.
	- [[Binaries]]: files in the graph, partly built.
